## Full description (4000 chars max)
Time, written in bone and blood.

Blackshield Clock is a gothic home-screen widget in the Blackshield canon:
the hour in bone-white blackletter, a single blood-red line, the date in
cold steel. Rendered with the bundled Pirata One typeface — it looks the
same on every launcher, every time.

**The craft**
- Bitmap-rendered face: gothic time, blood divider, letterspaced steel date
- Minute-accurate: self-rescheduling tick aligned to the minute, survives
  reboots and timezone changes
- Resizable 4x2 widget, fits any grid
- 97 KB. No internet. No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Ever.

Pairs with Blackshield Icons for the full dark-steel home screen.

Pay once. It's yours. No subscriptions, ever.
