# Privacy Policy — Blackshield Clock

**Effective date:** September 11, 2026

## The short version

Blackshield Clock collects **no data whatsoever**. It is a home-screen clock
widget: it draws the time and date onto a bitmap, on your device, using your
system clock. That is the entire app.

## What the app accesses

- **No network access** — no internet permission, no connections, ever.
- **No personal data** — no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting,
  or advertising IDs.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarm)** — used solely to wake the widget once
  per minute so the clock ticks on time. No alarm data leaves the device.
- **Boot receiver** — restarts the minute tick after your phone reboots.

## Third parties

None. No SDKs, no trackers, no ads.
